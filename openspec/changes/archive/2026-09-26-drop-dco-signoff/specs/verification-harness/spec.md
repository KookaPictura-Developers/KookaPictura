## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: DCO sign-off checked on push and pull request
**Reason**: The sign-off is a project convention, not a GPL-3.0 requirement, and
it does not cover employer IP the way a CLA does. The project drops the per-commit
requirement and its CI enforcement.
**Migration**: Contribute under the same GPL-3.0-or-later licence without a
`Signed-off-by:` trailer. The provenance attestation in `CONTRIBUTING.md` stays as
a condition of contributing; it no longer rides on a trailer. Existing commits
keep their trailers and history is not rewritten.
