# Contributing to Kooka Pictura

Thanks for helping. This project is an independent reimplementation of the
Adobe Photoshop CS6 feature set; because of that, the **provenance rules below
are not optional** — they are what keep the project defensible.

## License and sign-off (DCO)

Kooka Pictura is distributed under the **GPL-3.0-or-later** (see
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). By contributing you agree to license your contribution
under the same terms.

Every commit must carry a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

Use `git commit -s`. CI rejects pull requests whose commits lack a sign-off.
The sign-off certifies the [DCO 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/): you
wrote the change or have the right to submit it.

## Provenance attestation (required)

By signing off, you also attest that your contribution was **not** derived from
any of the following, and that you did not use them to inform it:

- Adobe source code, in any form — leaked, shared, or obtained under an SDK,
  beta, or partner agreement;
- decompilation or disassembly of Adobe binaries (Photoshop, plug-ins, `.8bf`,
  PSD/PSB tools, resource editors);
- information subject to an NDA, or knowledge from Adobe's plug-in/developer
  programs;
- knowledge you hold as a current or former Adobe employee that is not public.

If a behaviour is not sourced from public documentation or lawful black-box
observation, it is an **open question**, not a fact — mark it as such.

## Assets

- Do **not** add Adobe assets (profiles, fonts, brushes, patterns, gradients,
  swatches, actions, icons, sample files) or anything traced from them.
- Every new asset must be listed in [`assets/PROVENANCE.md`](assets/PROVENANCE.md)
  with its author, tool, and license.
- Test fixtures must be generated by the project's own generators
  (`scripts/generate-fixtures.py`, `crates/pictura-codec/examples/make_smart_fixtures.rs`),
  not extracted from a Photoshop-authored file. `guard.sh` and CI reject
  Adobe-tool creator strings in tracked binaries.

## Third-party dependencies

- New Rust or C++ dependencies require a stated reason and must pass the
  `deny.toml` allow-list (`cargo deny check`). Copyleft outside GPL/LGPL, and
  AGPL/SSPL/EUPL in particular, are rejected.
- After adding a dependency, regenerate the notices:
  `python3 scripts/third-party-licenses.py`.

## Before you open a PR

```bash
cargo fmt --all
cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings
bash scripts/verify-fast.sh          # fmt, clippy, tests, guard, openspec
cargo deny check                      # license/dependency gate
```

Keep diffs scoped, add one runnable check for non-trivial logic, and don't
change `docs/` without a `TASK-ALLOWS-DOCS` marker.

## Scope note

This is **not** a certified two-team independent-creation process. It is a documentation-first,
independently authored project built only from public sources; the separation
between the behavioural specification and the implementation is a target, not a
guarantee. See
[`docs/11-cross-cutting/adr-provenance-separation.md`](docs/11-cross-cutting/adr-provenance-separation.md)
and [`docs/00-overview/licensing-and-provenance.md`](docs/00-overview/licensing-and-provenance.md).
